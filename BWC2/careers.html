Site Sypervisor/Site Engineer
Taiping & Kamunting, Perak

We are looking for a Site Supervisor to oversee on-site construction activities, ensure project progress, uphold safety standards, and coordinate closely with project teams and workers.

Salary Range (RM): 2,500 - 4,500

Job Type: Full-time

Responsibilities:
  • Supervising and coordinating the activities of construction workers on-site to ensure tasks are completed on schedule and to the required quality standards.
  • Monitoring and reporting on progress, identifying and resolving any issues that may arise.
  • Ensuring compliance with relevant health, safety and environmental regulations.
  • Liaising with project managers, engineers and other stakeholders to communicate project updates and address any concerns.
  • Provide post-installation support for system commissioning and troubleshooting.
  • Providing guidance and support to the construction team so that project could progress in a timely manner.
  • Maintaining accurate records and documentation related to the construction project.
Qualifications:
  • Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Management, or related field.
  • At least 1 - 2 years of working experience in the related field is required.
  • Highly motivated with strong leadership, interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Excellent knowledge of construction details and relevant rules, regulations and quality standard.

Interested applicants, please submit your resume via email to hr@bwcconstruction.com.my . Only shortlisted candidates will be notified.

Machine Operator
Taiping & Kamunting, Perak

Seeking physically fit individuals to operate excavators for soil excavation and loading tasks, ensuring machine maintenance and site safety at Taiping & Kamunting.

Salary Range (RM): 3,000 - 5,000

Job Type: Full-time

Responsibilities:
  • Operate an excavator to dig soil at the specified water level.
  • Collect and manage soil slop produced during excavation.
  • Load soil into lorries for transport or disposal as instructed.
  • Perform daily inspections of the excavator to ensure the machine is in good working condition.
  • Apply grease to the machine 2–3 times a day to ensure smooth operation.
  • Follow safety procedures and maintain cleanliness at the worksite.
Qualifications:
  • Experience in operating excavator or bulldozer.
  • Minimum of 2 years experience is needed.
  • Candidate must be physically fit.
  • Male applicants are encouraged to apply.

Interested applicants, please submit your resume via email to hr@bwcconstruction.com.my . Only shortlisted candidates will be notified.
