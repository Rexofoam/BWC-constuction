Rantau Panjang

Rantau Panjang Project

After a severe landslide hit Jalan Lata Tinggi–Rantau Panjang in the Selama district, we executed a full-scale slope recovery operation. Our work involved reinforced soil slope construction, rocktoe installation, compacted fill layers, and geotextile applications for durability and effective drainage. Final touches included hydroseeding and biodegradable matting to encourage ecological restoration. This project underlines our proficiency in slope engineering and sustainable recovery strategies.
